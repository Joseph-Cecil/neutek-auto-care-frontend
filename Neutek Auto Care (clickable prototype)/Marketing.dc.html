Questions

Good to know before you book.

Do I need an account to book?

No. Give your name and phone number and we will confirm. An account lets you track cars, approve quotes and pay online.

Will you start work without asking me?

Never. Routine services have fixed prices. Anything else is quoted first, and nothing starts until you approve it.

How can I pay?

Mobile money, card or bank transfer, online or at the workshop.

Can I follow my car without signing in?

Yes. Enter your job number or plate on the tracking page and you will see the status and dates.

Do you serve company fleets?

Yes. Fleet accounts get credit terms, driver details and scheduled maintenance reminders.
